## 1. Problem Statement

Teams often write specifications as unstructured text, then discover only during
prototype or implementation that the idea is impossible, useless, more complex
than the baseline, or aimed at the wrong problem.

Basis exists to move that failure earlier.

+The validation surface is considered useful only when it traces a goal back to
+one of those early-failure modes: impossibility, uselessness, unjustified
+complexity, or missing the stated problem.
